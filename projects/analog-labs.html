ELEE 4270Where does the noise enter, and what rejects it.

Differential pairs and CMRR, cascode stages, MOSFET and op-amp behaviour, push-pull output stages. Simulated in LTspice, then built and measured.

The lasting value was not the topologies. It was the habit of asking, at every stage, where the noise enters and what rejects it. That question is why the Hydration Coach uses an instrumentation amplifier rather than a bare ADC read, and why the bench instrument's MOSFETs sit inside the feedback loop.

ELEE 4230A number without an error bar is not a measurement.

One lab: measure a potentiometer's shaft angle by using it to set the frequency of a 555 astable oscillator, then read that frequency on an ESP32 through interrupt-based period averaging. Resistance becomes frequency, frequency becomes a timestamp difference, and that difference becomes an angle.

The transfer function follows from the 555's internals. The capacitor always charges and discharges through exactly ln(2) time constants, because the internal comparators trip at one third and two thirds of Vcc. That is the origin of the familiar 1.44 factor: it is 1/ln(2).

The uncertainty analysis was the part that taught me something. Propagating component tolerances through with RSS partial derivatives showed that the capacitor dominates the error budget, and that total uncertainty is not constant across the range. It runs from roughly 1.7% at one end to 4% at the other, because sensitivity scales as 1/f². The measurement is meaningfully worse at one end of the sweep, and nothing in the schematic tells you that.
