2026Analog & EmbeddedTeam of 5 — I led it

Raspberry Pi Bench Instrument

Four instruments in one box, built under one hard rule: no instrumentation ICs. A Raspberry Pi has no analog input, so the voltmeter isn't a library call — it's a 6-bit successive-approximation ADC I built from an R-2R ladder and a comparator. I was project lead and electrical lead on a team of five, and designed the Class AB push-pull output stage, the voltmeter, and the ohmmeter.

Raspberry Pi · R-2R SAR ADC · TL071CP · IRFZ24N / IRF5210 · MCP4231 · LM311 · SPI · pigpio · Python

2026Analog Front EndSolo · V2 in progress

Instrumented Water Bottle

I get migraines, and they're linked to dehydration — so I built a bottle that notices when I've stopped drinking. Five sensors, a custom analog front end (Semitec thermistor in a Wheatstone bridge, INA121P at gain 2, Steinhart-Hart linearisation), a custom PCB, and a printed enclosure. Now being rebuilt as a cheap, bottle-agnostic keychain counter — because V1 was a good exercise and a bad product.

ESP32-C3 SuperMini · INA121P · Wheatstone bridge · Steinhart-Hart · SHT40 · LSM6DSO · A02YYUW · EasyEDA / JLCPCB · Fusion 360
