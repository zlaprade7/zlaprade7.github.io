001

JARVIS

A Raspberry Pi that runs my room. It reads temperature, humidity and motion, then acts on what it finds: turns the lights on when I walk in, sleeps the monitor over DDC/CI, tracks when I fall asleep, and puts my day on a screen I can read from across the room.

Every panel on that screen just reports a number, except one. The JARVIS panel takes the calendar, the weather, the sensors and my sleep, and turns them into a single sentence I can actually use. That's the part I care about.

The demo on the left is running right now. Go press the buttons.
