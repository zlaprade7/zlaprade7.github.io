JARVIS

A Raspberry Pi that measures the conditions in my room and acts on them: driving smart lighting, controlling the display over DDC/CI, tracking sleep, and running an always-on dashboard.

Every panel on that screen reports a number, except one. The JARVIS panel takes the calendar, the weather, the sensors and the sleep record and reduces them to a single actionable sentence. That panel is what makes it a system rather than a display.

Flask · 7 threads · SHT40 · PIR · WiZ over UDP · DDC/CI · systemd

Raspberry Pi Bench Instrument

Four instruments in one box, built under a single constraint: no instrumentation ICs. A Raspberry Pi has no analog input, so the voltmeter is a 6-bit successive-approximation ADC built from an R-2R ladder and a comparator.

I was project lead and electrical lead on a team of five, and designed the Class AB push-pull output stage, the voltmeter and the ohmmeter.

R-2R SAR ADC · TL071CP · IRFZ24N / IRF5210 · MCP4231 · 4-layer board

Hydration Coach

A water bottle that measures water level, ambient conditions and activity, then adjusts a daily hydration target accordingly. Five sensors, a Wheatstone bridge into an INA333 instrumentation amplifier, a custom two-layer board and a printed housing, built for $31.70 against a $50 cap.

Measured error came in at 0.15% to 0.28% on temperature and under 0.3% on water level.

ESP32-C3-MINI · INA333 · Semitec 103AT-2 · SHT40 · MPU-6050 · A02YYUW · Kalman and median filtering
